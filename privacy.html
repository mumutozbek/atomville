Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Atomville is a learning game played by children. We built it so that it collects as little as possible, and so that everything it does collect is something a parent would expect. This policy explains exactly what that means in plain language.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. If you do, nothing leaves the device.
  • There are no ads, no trackers, and no third-party analytics — none, ever.
  • We never ask a child for an email address, a phone number, a birthday, or a photo.
  • Signing in is optional and exists for one reason: to carry game progress to another device.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app, at any time.

Playing without an account

The game is fully playable without signing in. In this mode your progress — levels finished, stars, badges, and settings — is stored only on your own device. We cannot see it, and it is removed if you delete the app.

If you choose to sign in

Signing in is entirely optional. Its only purpose is to let a player continue on another device, such as moving from a phone to an iPad. If you sign in, we store the following:

What Why
An account identifier (a random ID) To know which saved progress belongs to you. It is not your name or email.
A display name So the app can greet the right player. See the note below on each sign-in method.
Game progress Levels completed, stars, badges, experience points, and difficulty setting.
Subscription status Whether a Ranger Club subscription is currently active, and when the period ends.

Sign in with Apple

We request only your name from Apple. We do not request your email address, so we never receive it. Apple also lets you choose not to share your real name.

Sign in with Google

Google provides your email address as part of signing in, and we use it as your display name. If you would rather we did not receive an email address at all, use Sign in with Apple instead, or play without signing in.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party analytics or behavioural tracking software.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No profiles built about a child, and no data sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No birthdays, phone numbers, home addresses, or school information.

Children

This app is designed to be attractive to children, so we deliberately hold ourselves to a stricter standard than the law alone requires. We do not show advertising, we do not profile players, and we do not ask children for personal information. Playing anonymously is a complete experience, not a limited one.

Purchases are protected by a grown-up check inside the app, and by Apple's own purchase controls (such as Ask to Buy and Screen Time restrictions), which we encourage every family to use.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information you would like removed, contact us using the address below and we will delete it.

Payments

All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your card number, billing address, or any other payment details. Apple tells us only whether a subscription is currently active. Apple's own privacy policy governs the payment itself.

Where your data is kept

Saved progress is stored in a database hosted by Supabase Inc. on our behalf. Data is encrypted while travelling over the internet, and access rules on the database mean a signed-in player can only ever read or change their own row.

How long we keep it

We keep saved progress for as long as your account exists, because that is the entire point of it. When you delete your account, the data is deleted with it.

Deleting your account and data

Open the app, go to Save Progress, and choose Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your account and all saved progress from our servers. It cannot be undone, and it does not require you to email anyone.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Subscriptions are managed in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. The in-app deletion described above satisfies the deletion right immediately. For anything else, contact us and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
