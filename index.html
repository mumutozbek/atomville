ATOMVILLEReactor Rangers · a STEM learning game

Learn how the lights stay on.

Atomville is an adventure game for curious kids. Players join the Reactor Rangers, run a real control room, and learn how a power plant actually works — reactors, coolant, backup power, grid synchronisation — by doing it, not by reading about it.

Built for families

  • No advertising and no tracking. Ever.
  • Playable without an account — nothing leaves the device.
  • Purchases sit behind a grown-up check.
  • Three campaign levels, two Discovery Park stations, and a patrol chapter are free.
