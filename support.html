Getting started

What is Atomville?

A third-person adventure that teaches how a power plant actually works — reactors, coolant, backup power, grid synchronisation — through missions a child plays rather than a lesson they read. It is aimed at curious kids and the adults who end up playing it with them.

What is free?

The first three campaign levels, the first two Discovery Park stations, and the first chapter of Ranger Patrols. No time limit and no advertising. That is a real slice of the game, not a demo.

Ranger Club subscription

What does it include?

Campaign levels 4–8, every Discovery Park station, all Ranger Patrol chapters, the badge wall and certificate — plus every new mission pack we release while your subscription is active.

What does it cost?

$4.99 per month or $29.99 per year, in US dollars; your local price is shown in the app. New subscribers start with a 3-day free trial. One subscription covers everyone in your Apple Family Sharing group.

How do I cancel?

On your device, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Atomville, and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. You can also reach this screen from the Ranger Club button on the game's title screen.

What happens to progress if I cancel?

Nothing is lost. Stars, badges and completed levels stay exactly as they were. The game simply returns to the free content, and everything reappears if you subscribe again.

I subscribed but the game still shows content as locked

Open the Ranger Club screen and tap Restore. That re-checks your subscription with Apple. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase, and that it has an internet connection.

I want a refund

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account. If something in the app went wrong, tell us too so we can fix it.

Accounts and progress

Do I need an account?

No. The game is fully playable without one, and progress is saved on your device. An account exists only so progress can follow a player to another device.

How do I move progress to a new device?

On the first device, tap Save Progress on the title screen and sign in. Do the same on the new device with the same account, and your progress appears. Signing in only ever adds progress — it never overwrites a better result with a worse one.

How do I delete my account?

Tap Save Progress, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all saved progress from our servers straight away. Note that it does not cancel a subscription — do that in Settings, as described above.

Playing the game

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. The game is played in landscape.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game runs on the device. An internet connection is only needed to sign in, sync progress, or start a subscription.

Is it safe for children?

Yes, by design. There is no advertising, no tracking, no chat, and no link out to social media. Purchases sit behind a grown-up check as well as Apple's own parental controls. Nuclear safety is presented honestly but calmly — the game says “training paused, let's try again”, never anything alarming.

I found a bug

Please tell us, and include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Bug reports from players are the fastest way things get fixed.
